About Ruby

A private space
in Dublin.

Ruby Spa is a discreet destination for sensory massage experiences, created for guests who value privacy, comfort and attentive service.

Our Space

Considered, quiet, private.

Every room is arranged to feel private and unhurried, with lighting, temperature and sound kept deliberately low-key so attention stays on the experience itself.

Our Approach

Paced around you.

A short consultation before each session allows pressure, pacing and focus to be adapted to how you feel that day. Communication stays open and comfortable throughout.

Our Values

Discretion, respect, attention.

These three principles shape every part of a visit — from how appointments are handled to how each session is delivered.

In Practice
